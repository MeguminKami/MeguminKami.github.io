About Me

Background

I recently completed my Master's in Electrical and Computer Engineering at FEUP. Over the last few years, I have built my experience through both academic and applied work, especially in robotics and industrial contexts.

I have almost two years of research experience in robotics, mainly focused on system automation and its application in the lithium battery field. I also spent six months at Bosch in Ovar, where I developed a simulation tool, which gave me valuable experience working in an industrial environment and contributing to practical engineering solutions.

Research Focus

My main interest is in PLC programming and industrial automation, which is the area I want to continue developing professionally. What motivates me most is the possibility of simplifying processes, improving efficiency, and creating reliable control solutions for real industrial systems.

Although most of my previous experience has been in robotics research and simulation rather than direct PLC work, that path strengthened my interest in automation and confirmed that this is the field where I want to grow.

Technical Skills

My technical background includes experience in automation systems, robotics, simulation, and control-oriented development. I have expertise in IEC 61131-3 programming in CODESYS and C programming for PLC control, and I am especially interested in applying these skills in industrial automation contexts.

Even though I have not yet had the opportunity to work in a role fully centered on PLC programming, In addition I have a large portfolio of projects in Python, C++, and Arduino, which demonstrates my ability to learn new technologies and apply them effectively.

Built with AI assistance

Brainstorming, code assistance & writing refinement via GPT Image Creation Claude Codex DeepWrite but all decisions & implementation by João Ferreira.

This website was built with the help of AI and that is part of the experiment and fun. AI is a great tool for testing ideas, speeding up the coding process, and adding a bit of fun to lighter projects. But when it comes to original thought, real creativity, and meaningful content, it still cannot replace a human mind.

That is why my work stays at the center of everything here. Eventhough sometimes I used AI to help to fully develop applications, I made sure to review, edit, and guide every step of the process.

Furthermore for important projects, I prefer to guide it myself using AI as an assitante to create code snippets, brainstorm ideas, and to refine functions and methods. I find that this way I can maintain full control over the quality and direction of the work, while still benefiting from AI's ability to speed up certain tasks and provide inspiration.
